\begin{abstract}
This paper proposes a novel deep learning approach for bearing Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction that combines multi-scale CNN feature extraction, cross-modal attention mechanism, dual-path LSTM for temporal modeling, and a stage-aware PHM loss function. The proposed method effectively captures both short-term dynamics and long-term trends in bearing degradation while addressing the asymmetric nature of prediction errors in prognostic tasks. Experimental results on PRONOSTIA and XJTU-SY datasets demonstrate superior performance compared to existing state-of-the-art methods.
\end{abstract}
